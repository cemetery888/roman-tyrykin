Dobovo

Redesign of a popular short-term apartment rental service.

Travel

B2C

Company

Turum-burum

Role

UX Designer

Dobovo ↗ is the leading website for short-term apartment rentals in Ukraine, serving as the main competitor to Airbnb in the country.

71

Cities across the country

6,000,000+

Nights booked

7,400+

Apartments for all tastes

120,000+

Reviews written

Results and metrics

  • The apartment comparison feature is now used four times more often.
  • The average time spent viewing apartment pages increased by 27% (ATV +27%).
  • Completed bookings rose by 35% (CB +35%).
  • Booking conversion on mobile devices increased by 12% (CR +12%).

Problems

  • The site was not responsive but had a mobile version that lacked many important features available in the full version. For example, the mobile version did not even have a user profile page.
  • Using filters when searching for apartments was not very convenient — information was poorly grouped, some important filters were missing, while competitors had them.
  • Users did not stay on apartment pages — they often left without starting a booking.
  • Users frequently did not complete the booking process.
  • The unique apartment comparison feature, which competitors do not have, was used by very few people.
  • Overall, the UI was outdated (high information density, lack of consistency, clashing colors, etc.).

Solution

A complete redesign of the site based on a Mobile First concept, using research data and information obtained from the product owner.

Why a complete redesign instead of a targeted one?

The site consisted of two versions — desktop and mobile — which was technically inefficient for this type of product and complicated for the team's resources. All functionality could be implemented in a single responsive version of the site. It was clear that this required a complete redesign, which we undertook.

Why Mobile First?

The number of visitors to the site from mobile devices was already higher at the start of our work on the product, and their numbers were growing. With this trend, it makes sense to design for mobile devices as the primary focus.

What I did

  • Participated in online and offline meetings with stakeholders to identify requirements.
  • Reviewed Google Analytics data and utilized it in our work.
  • Analyzed competitors.
  • Created prototypes of key pages of the site.
  • Reorganized filters in search results (list of apartments in the city), making them significantly easier to use — this was evident from metrics (the time from entering the site to transitioning to booking was significantly reduced) and a decrease in support inquiries.
  • Simplified apartment comparison — a unique feature of Dobovo that competitors, including Airbnb, still do not have. Comparing apartments became easier, thus speeding up the selection process among a vast number of options.

Approach

Together with the Dobovo team, we decided to follow this approach.

1. Initial assessment

We study Google Analytics data, analyze the current site and competitor sites, and ultimately prepare a long list of questions.

2. Information gathering

We conduct group calls with the product team to clarify everything that interests us.

3. Presentation of prototypes

We iteratively design the interface first for mobile devices and present the prototypes to the owner.

4. More design

Upon approval of the prototypes, we begin designing the interface for tablets and desktops while simultaneously working on the UI.

The product team consists of experienced professionals who possess comprehensive information about the product and its users. Communication with the Dobovo team became the main source of knowledge for the successful implementation of the project.

We and the product owner conducted dozens of group calls and several offline meetings, asking a huge number of questions and receiving answers. All the information obtained was documented, and we constantly referred to it during the work.

Competitors

I analyzed Ukrainian competitors and similar products in the global market and highlighted interesting solutions on their sites.

Then, together with the product owner, we decided which of their solutions would be included in the new design.

Search results (list of apartments in the city)

The search results page is one of the key pages on the site — in the overwhelming majority of cases, the selection of apartments occurs precisely there.

Changes to current filters

  • I separated the accommodation type filters (entire property, private room, shared room) and type of housing (apartment, house, hostel, hotel) from the number of rooms selection (it was unclear what they were doing there), thus creating two separate filters.
  • The additional filters mainly contained amenities. I moved them to the amenities filter, which already contained some amenities.
  • I simplified the price selection per night by adding a slider for value selection (this is easier than manually entering prices from and to).
  • Next to each filter, I indicated how many such apartments are available for booking.

New filters

  • Nearest metro (if available in the selected city)
  • Streets
  • District (previously only microdistrict)
  • Attractions

According to support information, users of the site repeatedly requested the addition of these filters.

Filtering by apartment attributes

Previously, there was no option to filter by instant booking, no prepayment, and expert owner. According to support, these attributes, especially the first two, can be crucial when selecting apartments.

Displaying all found apartments on the map

Previously, it was only possible to view the location on the map for a specific apartment; I suggested adding the ability to display all apartments from the search results on the map — this makes it easier to make a choice if location is important.

Booking statistics for selected dates

I added information on what percentage of apartments are booked for the selected dates. If the percentage is high, it may prompt the user to hurry to make a booking.

Adjacent dates and booking statistics for them

I also indicated the booking percentage for adjacent dates — this will allow users to easily select the nearest days if they cannot find suitable accommodation for the originally specified dates.

Apartment comparison

The ability to compare apartments is a unique feature for sites of this type. Dobovo's competitors, including Airbnb, still do not have this feature.

Problems

Despite the usefulness of the feature, only a small portion of users utilized it. I suspected the reasons were as follows:

Lack of date selection for check-in and check-out, and consequently, the inability to compare by price per night, which is a primary factor for a large number of users.

Poorly structured information about the compared apartments, without division into blocks. Visually, the information was difficult to perceive.

Solution

I added date selection for stays. It became possible to show prices per night.

I grouped information about apartments. City and district - in location, types and number of sleeping places - in sleeping places, etc.

Apartment page

House rules (check-in and check-out times, various restrictions, deposit, key collection, etc.) I extracted from the general description of the apartments and placed in a separate block. This information is critically important for some users, so hiding it was not entirely logical.

House rules (check-in and check-out times, various restrictions, deposit, key collection, etc.) I extracted from the general description of the apartments and placed in a separate block. This information is critically important for some users, so hiding it was not entirely logical.

I added cancellation policies. Previously, users had to contact the apartment owners to clarify this information. Not all owners could respond quickly to inquiries, so in some cases, users simply booked other apartments.

I added a list of offers so that users could easily see which booking dates are the most advantageous.

I added a share function. Social networks and messengers are evolving, so having such a feature is simply essential.

Booking

Despite the fact that the booking process was already well established, users often did not complete it and left the site. I did not have precise information about the reasons for this; I could only speculate.

The product team was hesitant to completely overhaul the booking process, so it was decided to make only some changes based on common sense and best practices.

I added house rules to the first step of booking, as they contain important information, especially check-in and check-out times. This way, users do not need to interrupt the booking process if they need to review the house rules again.

I made the use of gift certificates an option. Previously, it was a separate payment method, which was extremely inconvenient, especially when the certificate amount was lower than the booking cost — in such cases, the certificates were simply useless. It is also worth noting that Dobovo gift certificates were very popular, so the situation needed urgent correction.

As a result of the changes made, the number of completed bookings increased by 35%.

Personal account

This part of the product was completely absent in the mobile version of the site. I reviewed the personal account, created prototypes of all states, and handed them over to the designer for UI work.

Now the profile and all functions within it are accessible on any device.

Current status

The product is successfully operating and developing. At the end of 2019, Dobovo announced the beginning of cooperation with LUN — the largest player in the apartment search market. Thanks to this partnership, the short-term rental market has reached a new level and helps Ukrainians and foreign guests travel more frequently and comfortably within the country.
